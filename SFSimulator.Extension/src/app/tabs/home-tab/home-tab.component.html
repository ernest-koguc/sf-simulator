Welcome to SF Lab!

SF Lab is an unofficial, independently developed companion tool for Shakes & Fidget, facilitating overall game progression. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Playa Games GmbH.

Getting started

Dungeons tab shows win ratio vs currently available dungeons based on your character live statistics. Any updates to your character are tracked and refresh the simulation.

Dailies tab is meant as a reminder TO-DO list of in-game activities like wheel of fortune, arena fights and so on.
